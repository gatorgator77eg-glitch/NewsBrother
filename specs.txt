A political map news tool organizes current events left-to-right by political leaning, utilizing a split-column browser interface with a central search bar, color-coded bias indicators, and balanced multi-source comparison views.User Experience (UX) & User Interface (UI) DesignLayout Structure:Top Anchor: Centered persistent search bar with a prominent "Map the News" action button and quick-filter tag pills (e.g., Economy, Elections, Foreign Policy).Three-Column Canvas: Left pane for Progressive/Left-leaning coverage, Center pane for Neutral/Fact-focused wire services, and Right pane for Conservative/Right-leaning coverage.Visual Weight: Card-based story layout where headline font sizes scale dynamically based on story reach and source credibility scores.Color System & Accessibility:Left Column Accent: Cool blue/teal tint (#E3F2FD) for left-leaning publication badges.Center Column Accent: Neutral slate/gray tint (#F1F3F4) for center/factual reporting.Right Column Accent: Warm coral/red tint (#FFEBEE) for right-leaning publication badges.Contrast Check: WCAG AAA compliant text colors to ensure high legibility across split panels.Interactive Micro-Gestures:Hover Sync: Hovering over an article card in one column highlights corresponding coverage or missing blindspots in the opposing columns by 40%.Side-by-Side Drawer: Clicking "Compare Framing" slides open a bottom or side split drawer showing exact headline and subtext discrepancies side-by-side.Business RequirementsData Ingestion & Processing Engine:Source Aggregation: Real-time ingestion pipelines pulling from 50,000+ global RSS feeds, APIs, and verified independent outlets.NLP Bias Classification: Automated Large Language Model (LLM) classification engine mapping publishers to Left, Lean-Left, Center, Lean-Right, and Right spectrums using data from accredited media watchdogs (like AllSides or Ad Fontes).Core Functional Modules:Search & Topic Clustering: Grouping disparate headlines talking about the exact same real-time event into a single unified "Story Node" distributed across the political spectrum map.Blindspot Alert System: Automated notification flags when a major story is covered exclusively by one side of the political spectrum with zero coverage on the other.Performance & Scalability:Latency Target: Sub-800ms query response time for search results mapped across the three-column layout.Data Freshness: Continuous background cron jobs updating breaking news threads every 5 minutes.
