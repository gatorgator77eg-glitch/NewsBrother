Business Requirements Document (BRD)Project Name: Project Janus – Predictive Global Intelligence EngineDocument Version: 1.0 (Production-Ready)Target Audience: Executive Leadership, Product Owners, Investment Strategy Teams, Development Leads1. Executive Summary & ObjectiveProject Janus is a next-generation deep research and market intelligence platform. It fuses real-time global media narratives with a 10-year quantitative market database. Unlike traditional tools that look at news and stock prices in isolation, Janus treats narrative as a leading indicator of market liquidity, volatility, and price direction.The primary business objective is to provide institutional portfolio managers and investment analysts with an information advantage. It accomplishes this by identifying structural market turning points, geopolitical disruptions, and valuation anomalies 48 to 72 hours before they register on traditional quantitative tickers.2. Core Business Value PropositionsNarrative Arbitrage: Spotting valuation gaps caused by media bias, information asymmetry, or delayed translation across international markets.Early Warning System: Quantifying geopolitical and regulatory risks into actionable leading market signals.Backtested Sentiment Validation: Eradicating market "hype" by validating current media trends against 10 years of historical asset price reactions.3. Innovative Functional Requirements3.1. The "Echo Chamber" Cross-Border Arbitrage EngineBusiness Objective: Exploit the temporal delay in information flow between primary global markets (US, China) and regional production hubs (Brazil, Argentina, Eastern Europe).Business Capabilities:The system must monitor localized global news domains (e.g., ://eastmoney.com, bursa.ro) in their native text.The system must calculate a Narrative Velocity Score (NVS). This tracks how fast a localized financial story propagates across geographies and across the political spectrum (from the 52 configured RSS feeds).The system must trigger an automated Arbitrage Alert when a high-impact localized narrative emerges regarding supply chains or regulations before that topic registers in mainstream Western media or moves Western ticker prices.3.2. Geopolitical Shockwave BacktesterBusiness Objective: Transform qualitative global developments into an objective, backtestable risk index to optimize defensive portfolio positioning.Business Capabilities:Users must be able to input or highlight any current media narrative cluster (e.g., "South American export tariff shifts" or "European energy supply changes").The tool must search the 10-year GDELT and historical archive to find past historical periods with matching media footprints.The system must automatically analyze the historical OHLCV data of the 5,082 core tickers during those historical matching windows.The tool must generate a Shockwave Impact Report displaying historical max drawdowns, sector-by-sector performance, volatility spikes, and median recovery times under those specific narrative conditions.3.3. Corporate Amnesia & Promise TrackerBusiness Objective: Protect long-term equity portfolios from corporate spin and greenwashing by auditing historical executive rhetoric against hard equity pricing.Business Capabilities:The system must compile historical public statements, guidance, and strategic goals extracted from the media archive for tickers across major indices (S&P 500, NASDAQ-100, FTSE 100, Nikkei 225, DAX, NYSE).The system must continuously match past public executive narrative benchmarks against the subsequent 10-year OHLCV price progression.The tool must calculate a dynamic Corporate Credibility Rating (CCR) for every ticker, flagging "Narrative Whiplash"—where executive media messaging fundamentally disconnects from long-term capital preservation and price performance.3.4. Media-Driven Volatility & Liquidity RadarBusiness Objective: Mitigate liquidity risk and capital lock-up by identifying sentiment polarization before trading volume dries up or explodes.Business Capabilities:The tool must process the 52 RSS feeds to measure media consensus versus ideological polarization on specific sectors or tickers.When a ticker's media coverage crosses a defined polarization threshold, the system must cross-reference historical price rows to evaluate if that specific profile typically precedes a liquidity crunch or an extreme price swing.The system must output a predictive Liquidity Risk Flag to caution traders before executing large block orders in assets entering a media hyper-cycle.3.5. Automated Macro Paradigm-Shift DetectorBusiness Objective: Automate macro asset allocation transitions by identifying structural economic regime shifts through linguistic drift.Business Capabilities:The platform must run continuous semantic auditing across the global daily news footprint to identify systemic, subtle changes in macro terminology (e.g., transitioning from "aggressive expansion" to "margin preservation" or "inventory drawdown").The system must overlay these narrative life-cycles onto the 10-year global index data to pinpoint exactly which phase of the macroeconomic cycle the global markets are transitioning into.The system must automatically generate a Strategic Reallocation Directive showing which asset classes and geographic indices (e.g., shifting weights from NASDAQ-100 to defensive DAX or FTSE tickers) historically outperformed under the newly detected narrative paradigm.4. User Experience (UX) & Dashboard Specifications4.1. The Janus Command CenterThe Global Narrative Heatmap: A visual matrix plotting the Top Archive Countries (US, China, Brazil, Argentina, UK) against market sectors. Color intensity must represent Narrative Velocity, while directional symbols (up/down arrows) represent correlated historical price trends over the 10-year history.Ticker Divergence Window: A dedicated interface displaying a ranked list of tickers experiencing the highest divergence between Media Heat and Price Stability. This acts as a hunting ground for portfolio managers looking for mispriced assets.The Paradigm Dial: A prominent dashboard graphic displaying the current macroeconomic regime as dictated by the Automated Macro Paradigm-Shift Detector, paired with a one-click button to export the optimized index allocation weights.4.2. Deep Research SandboxNatural Language Query Interface: A non-technical, simple search bar where analysts can type questions like: "How do Brazilian agricultural tickers perform when Chinese financial media heavily discusses import restrictions?"Interactive Timeline View: A dual-axis chart engine that matches the historical volume of articles on a specific topic (X-axis) directly against the 10-year daily OHLCV rows (Y-axis) of any chosen ticker, allowing visual confirmation of narrative leads and price lags.5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)The success and business return on investment (ROI) of Project Janus will be evaluated based on the following specific business metrics:Alpha Generation Rate: The percentage of outperformance achieved by portfolios utilizing Janus's Strategic Reallocation Directives versus standard benchmark buy-and-hold indexing (e.g., S&P 500 or MSCI World).Signal Lead Time: Maintaining an average buffer of >36 hours between an "Echo Chamber" Arbitrage Alert trigger and the corresponding price movement in the target asset ticker.Analyst Time-to-Insight: Reducing the average time an investment analyst spends researching a geopolitical event and mapping its historical market precedents from hours down to under 60 seconds.Volatility Mitigation (Max Drawdown Protection): The measurable reduction in portfolio drawdown during major geopolitical shocks or market-wide corrections by utilizing the early warnings of the Volatility & Liquidity Radar.7.
